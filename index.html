CardioOncoPredict

Can the heart’s rhythm warn of chemotherapy damage early?

Anthracycline chemotherapy cures cancer but can injure the heart. This open research tool measures microvolt T-wave alternans — a T wave that changes every other beat by a few microvolts — in an ECG recording. Whether it appears before the heart is damaged is a hypothesis no published study has tested yet. This project is built to test it.

Intended use: education and signal-processing research. Upload one lead of a public or properly de-identified ECG — for example from PhysioNet — as EDF/EDF+ or plain CSV/TXT, at least 1–2 minutes long.
