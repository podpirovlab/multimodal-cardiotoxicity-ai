Problem

The heart is injured quietly, and we notice late

Anthracyclines (doxorubicin, epirubicin) remain a backbone of treatment for breast cancer, lymphomas, sarcomas and childhood leukaemia. Their side effect is dose-dependent injury to cardiomyocytes, which barely regenerate.

Among 2,625 patients treated with anthracyclines, about 9% developed cardiotoxicity, and 98% of those cases appeared within the first year (Cardinale et al., Circulation, 2015). The earlier it is found and cardioprotection starts, the more often heart function recovers.

Standard monitoring is echocardiography (LV ejection fraction) plus biomarkers (troponin, NT-proBNP), per the 2022 ESC guidelines. Ejection fraction drops only after tissue is already lost. An ECG is cheap, takes minutes and can be recorded continuously, but the changes are too small for the eye.

Project hypothesis: electrical repolarisation abnormalities, including microvolt T-wave alternans, appear before mechanical ones and can be extracted with mathematics.

Method

Five steps from electrode to number

The steps run in order; each uses the output of the previous one. The same algorithm is implemented in Python (cardioonco/) and JavaScript (assets/js/dsp.js) and is covered by tests.

1

Sampling and calibration

Electrodes measure a potential difference on the skin (about 1 mV). An ADC turns it into numbers at rate fs. By the Nyquist–Shannon theorem the rate must exceed twice the highest signal frequency.

V[n] = (Xraw[n] − baseline) / gain mV
tn = n / fs, fs > 2·fmax
2

Zero-phase filtering

A 0.5–40 Hz Butterworth filter removes breathing drift and muscle noise. Running it forward and backward (filtfilt) cancels the phase shift, so the ST-T shape is preserved.

|H(f)|² = 1 / (1 + (f/fc)2N)
y = reverse(H · reverse(H · x)) → phase = 0
3

R-peak detection (Pan–Tompkins)

A 5–15 Hz band isolates the steep QRS edges, derivative and squaring amplify them, a 150 ms integrator smooths them. An adaptive threshold and a 250 ms refractory period give one peak per beat.

d[n] = x[n+1] − x[n−1]
e[n] = d[n]², m[n] = (1/W)·Σ e[n−k], W = 150 ms
4

Spectral Method for TWA

128 beats are aligned on the R peak. For every point of the ST-T window we take the beat-to-beat series of values and compute its spectrum. Every-other-beat alternation peaks at exactly 0.5 cycles/beat, because e−iπn = (−1)n.

P(f) = ⟨ |(1/N)·Σn sk[n]·e−2πifn|² ⟩k
Valt = √(P(0.5) − μnoise)
K = (P(0.5) − μnoise) / σnoise
5

Decision and cross-check

Alternans is significant when Valt ≥ 1.9 µV and K ≥ 3. The Modified Moving Average (MMA) checks it independently: two templates, for even and odd beats, are updated with step 1/8.

An = An−1 + clip((beatn − An−1)/8, ±32 µV)
TWAMMA = max |A − B| / 2
Neural network

ECG and patient data: bilinear fusion

The same ECG means different things for a 25-year-old and a 70-year-old. So the network does not just concatenate features; it multiplies every ECG feature by every clinical feature.

Appending a 1 to each vector means the product contains pairwise interactions, ECG-only terms, metadata-only terms and a bias all at once (Tensor Fusion, Zadeh et al., 2017). The model has about 573 thousand parameters and fits on a smartwatch after ONNX export.

Training uses PTB-XL (PhysioNet): 21,799 clinical ECGs from 18,869 patients labelled by cardiologists. The STTC class (ST-T changes) is the closest to the electrical phenotype of cardiotoxicity. PTB-XL has no chemotherapy data, so this validates the method on real ECGs; it is not a cardiotoxicity detector.

Limitations

What the project cannot do yet

No chemotherapy labels

Open ECG datasets labelled for cardiotoxicity barely exist. The link between TWA and anthracyclines is a hypothesis to be tested on a clinical cohort.

TWA needs long recordings

The Spectral Method needs 128 beats (about 2 minutes), ideally during exercise. A standard 10-second ECG has about 12 beats.

Not a medical device

No clinical validation and no clearance. Results must not inform dosing or treatment.
