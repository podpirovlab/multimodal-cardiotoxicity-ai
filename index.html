Cardio-oncology · signal processing

Hear the heart before it weakens

Anthracycline chemotherapy cures cancer but can injure the heart, often before an echo shows it. This tool looks for the earliest electrical trace of that injury in a real ECG you upload: a T wave that alternates by a few microvolts, beat after beat.

Upload a recording How it works, in full

Research prototype, not a medical device. No clinical validation, no regulatory clearance (FDA, CE). Do not use this for diagnosis or treatment decisions.

The signal is filtered (0.5–40 Hz) and R peaks are found with the Pan–Tompkins algorithm. The ST-T window adapts to heart rate.

4 · Beat-series spectrum (Spectral Method)

noise band 0.44–0.490.5 cycles/beat

Alternating every other beat is an oscillation at exactly 0.5 cycles per beat. If the right-most point rises above the grey noise band, alternans is present.
